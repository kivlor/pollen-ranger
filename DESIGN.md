# Pollen Ranger

> First-Person MDK + Metroid Prime + Garden Reclamation

## The Pitch

You are a thumb-sized woodland scout (a seed-pod golem with acorn armor) dropped into an enormous, overgrown greenhouse that has fallen under a creeping gray rot ("The Blight").

## Core Mechanics

### 1. Dandelion Sniper (MDK-Style)
- **Arm-cannon** shoots high-velocity seed capsules
- **Bamboo scope** for long-range tagging of blighted fruit
- **Water droplets** can be shot onto dry soil to spawn sunflower trampolines

### 2. Aero-Gliding
- Deploy a **dandelion fluff parachute** (MDK ribbon-chute)
- Float across gaps between hanging flower pots
- Ride thermal updrafts to reach greenhouse rafters

### 3. Biome Reclamation (Metroidvania Progression)
- Purge **Queen Weevil** bosses to cleanse sectors
- Sunlight pierces glass, opening new paths
- Unlock new wings: Orchid Tropics, Cactus Desert, Compost Catacombs

## Visual Style

- **Scale**: Everything is HUGE from the player's thumb-sized perspective
- **Blight**: Gray, ash-like corruption spreading across surfaces
- **Bloom**: Lush greens, vibrant flowers, particle effects when purified
- **Lighting**: God rays through greenhouse glass, bloom on cleansed areas

## Technical Notes

- First-person 3D controller
